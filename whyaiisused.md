# 🤖 Why AI Is at the Core of KLA

---

## The Problem AI Solves

> Indian creators spend **4–6 hours** manually repurposing one video for different platforms.
> KLA compresses that into **under 60 seconds** — using AI end-to-end.

---

## Where AI Works in the Pipeline

```
Upload Video
    ↓
🎙️  AWS Transcribe     →  Converts speech to text with high accuracy
    ↓
👁️  AWS Rekognition    →  Reads visuals: scenes, objects, expressions
    ↓
🧠  AWS Bedrock        →  Detects content domain (Food, Tech, Finance...)
    ↓
⚡  Parallel Agents    →  7 Claude agents run simultaneously per platform
    ↓
📈  Viral Predictor    →  Scores content virality before publishing (0–100)
    ↓
✅  Creator Reviews    →  Human-in-the-loop approval before anything goes live
```

---

## What Each AI Layer Does

| AI Service | What It Does | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **AWS Transcribe** | Speech → text | Foundation for everything downstream |
| **AWS Rekognition** | Video scene & object analysis | Adds visual context to AI prompts |
| **AWS Bedrock** | Domain detection + content generation | Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Haiku per platform |
| **Viral Score Engine** | Predicts hook strength, pacing, engagement | Creator knows impact *before* publishing |
| **Domain Agents** | 9 personas (Tech, Food, Finance, Travel…) | Content feels native to each niche |

---

## The 3 Big AI Advantages

### ⚡ Speed
One video → 7 platform-ready content packs in **< 60 seconds**
Manual equivalent: **4–6 hours**

### 🎯 Domain Intelligence
AI detects the creator's niche and assigns a **domain-specific persona** (e.g. a Finance expert vs. a Food influencer) — so content sounds authentic, not generic.

### 🔀 Parallel Agents
All 7 platforms run as **independent Claude agents simultaneously** — each with its own model, prompt, and tone.
No serial bottleneck. No one-size-fits-all output.
