### Backup Plans

**If live demo fails:**
1. **Pre-recorded video:** Have a polished screen recording ready
2. **Cached results:** Show pre-processed video from cache
3. **Slides with screenshots:** Walk through with static images

**If internet fails:**
4. **Local demo mode:** Use GitHub Copilot + mocks (no AWS)
5. **Offline slides:** Full presentation without demo

**If time runs over:**
6. **Skip architecture slide:** Go straight to impact
7. **Shorten problem section:** 10s instead of 20s

---

Use these phrases to stick in judges' minds:

2. **"Domain intelligence, not generic AI"**
3. **"Watch AI think in real-time"**
4. **"$0.30 per video, not $50 per month"**

### Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
- Apologize for anything ("Sorry, this is slow...")
- Show bugs or errors
- Go over time (judges will cut you off)
- Use jargon without explanation
- Read from slides (talk to judges)
- Skip the demo (it's the wow moment)

✅ **Do:**
- Smile and make eye contact
- Speak clearly and confidently
- Show enthusiasm (you believe in this!)
- Handle questions gracefully
- Thank the judges

### Judge Q&A Preparation

**Top 10 Questions & Killer Answers:**

**Q1: "How accurate is your domain detection?"**
**A:** "95% accuracy on our test set of 50 videos across 4 domains. We use Claude 3.5's reasoning capabilities combined with transcript and visual analysis. If confidence is below 80%, we fall back to generic mode and ask the user to confirm."

**Q2: "What's your AWS cost per video?"**
**A:** "$0.30 per 5-minute video. Breakdown: Transcribe $0.12, Bedrock $0.15, Rekognition $0.02, S3/DynamoDB $0.01. We cache everything with 24-hour TTL, so repeat processing is free."

**Q3: "How do you handle multiple languages?"**
**A:** "We use Bedrock's multilingual capabilities with cultural adaptation. For example, translating a food video to Hindi preserves cooking terminology and adapts measurements. We support 10+ Indian languages."

**Q4: "What if the AI generates bad content?"**
**A:** "Human-in-the-loop workflow. Every output requires user approval. They can edit, regenerate, or reject. We also show confidence scores and explain reasoning. The AI is a co-pilot, not autopilot."

**Q5: "How does this scale to 1 million users?"**
**A:** "AWS managed services auto-scale. Bedrock and Transcribe are serverless. DynamoDB uses on-demand pricing. We'd add horizontal scaling for our Node.js backend behind an ALB. Caching reduces costs by 70%."

**Q6: "What's your competitive advantage?"**
**A:** "Domain intelligence. Generic AI tools don't understand that a food video needs a recipe card, or an education video needs study notes. We're the first platform with domain-specific content intelligence."

**Q7: "How long did this take to build?"**
**A:** "6 days for the hackathon. We used GitHub Copilot for free local testing and only used AWS for final validation and demo. Smart caching kept us under $80 budget."

**Q8: "What's your business model?"**
**A:** "Freemium. Free tier: 10 videos/month. Pro tier: $19/month unlimited. Agency tier: $99/month with team features. Target: YouTubers with 10K-1M subscribers."

**Q9: "What's next after the hackathon?"**
**A:** "User testing with 10 creators, add podcast support, improve domain detection, build mobile app. Goal: 1,000 users in 3 months."

**Q10: "Why should we pick you to win?"**
**A:** "Three reasons: (1) Technical excellence - working demo with advanced features, (2) Real impact - solves a $10B problem for 50M creators, (3) Execution - we shipped in 6 days what others take months to build."

## Key Insights from Presenter

### What Judges Love
- **Working demo:** Not slides, not mockups, WORKING software
- **Clear problem:** Quantified pain point
- **Wow moments:** 3+ moments that make them say "whoa"
- **Technical depth:** Show you know what you're doing
- **Business viability:** This could be a real company

### What Judges Hate
- **Bugs in demo:** Practice 10+ times
- **Vague problem:** "People need better tools" ❌
- **No demo:** Just slides ❌
- **Going over time:** Respect the clock
- **Can't answer questions:** Know your tech
